## How the two halves meet

Admin builds these first, so Studio builds against something real.

1. **The month tables**: `content_months` (month, brand set, status draft/published) and `content_month_posts` (date, tied to the day or not, type, content pillar, caption, how to edit, reel prompt, platforms, template id). Studio reads only published months.
2. **One access check**: a Worker route, `/access/me`, that says what a member gets: calendar months, library reach, scheduling, Done For You, course levels. It's worked out on the server from either source: a TEG brand paying for them, or their own card. Studio never decides access in the browser.

### What `/access/me` returns

GET with the member's Bearer token:

```
{ ok, level: "free" | "calendar" | "scheduling" | "done_for_you",
  staff, teg, edition, editions,      // edition = which month's posts: property | prestige | lettings | independent
  source: "staff" | "none" (later "teg" | "card"),
  calendar, preview,                  // preview = free member: show the month blurred
  library: { from, to } | null,       // months they can open, inclusive; from null = every month
  scheduling, done_for_you,
  course_levels: [...] }              // the guides.level values they can open
```

Staff can add `?as=free` (or any level) to see the hub as that level.

In Stripe: two prices, and the subscription keeps the one it started on.
- **Done For You is paid in advance, on the 21st (Danielle, 11 Nov; moved from the 20th so the cancel-by date comes first):** Done For You is the exception to billing on the 1st. It's taken on the **21st for the next month**, before the team starts that month's posts, which are delivered at the end of the month. Whoever joins mid-month doesn't get that month: their first posts are the next month's. The reason is that a payment taken on the 1st could fail after the posts were already made, which would leave us chasing a debt for work we'd already delivered. Paid on the 21st, nothing is made until it's paid. This fits the cancel-by-the-20th rule, because cancelling by the 20th means the next charge is never taken. Build it into everything for Done For You: Stripe, the staff queue, the waitlist and sign-up wording, the terms and the emails. The rules for the first charge are under Waiting on Danielle.
- **Done For You failed payment (Danielle, 11 Nov):** a failed payment on the 21st gives them about **10 days, until the 1st**, to pay. On the day it fails, we email them and ring the bell with a link to update their card. Stripe tries again during those 10 days. **We hold their posts: the team doesn't make any of them while a payment has failed.** As soon as we're told a payment has failed, the staff queue shows that member as "Payment failed, on hold". If it's paid by the 1st, the posts go ahead. If it isn't paid by the 1st, that month's posts aren't made, so we've never delivered anything that isn't paid for and there's nothing to chase. The member is flagged for staff to speak to them **manually**, so it doesn't end on its own. They keep the current month, because it was paid on the last 21st.
