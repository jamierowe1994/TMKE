What happens when you press Gift this pack

  1. The pack goes into their library straight away, at no charge, and shows under Pack orders › Gifted with your message beside it.
  2. The email goes out at once, with your message in it if you wrote one.
  3. Has an account: the notification is in their bell at once.
  4. No account yet: the email asks them to sign up with that address. The pack is matched to the address, so it's waiting in their library, and the notification arrives the first time they sign in.
  5. Nothing about a gift counts in the orders, Revenue or the dashboard.
