# Prompts still needing a brief

Everything here shows a member a one-line hook and, at most, a one-line hint.
What they do not yet have is the fuller guidance you wrote for September to
December — what the post is about, what to photograph, what the copy should
cover.

Fill in the **Brief** column in your own words and I will put them straight into
`src/data/content-calendar.js`. The Hook is what the card shows; change that too
if it does not sound like you.
